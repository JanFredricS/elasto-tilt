# Pendulum Mill crossings

The fourth campaign map retains its ID, six apples, upper-gallery door and curved wall route. Both lower gaps now span 5 m (4.975 m of clear collision space after the terrain beams' small join overlaps). Each cradle remains 2.8 m wide, leaving over 2 m of horizontal transfer that the seat cannot fill while stationary. These gaps remain narrower than the Hanging Garden's 7 m chasm.

The first suspension is 6 m long with damping 15; the second is 7.5 m long with damping 16. Both seats have mass 20. Their anchors put the unloaded seats at y = 2.5 when vertical, below the y = 3 banks, so the tilted ends meet the banks without presenting a tall boarding step. Initial angles of −0.12 radians leave both seats clear of the banks. The longer second suspension has the slower angular response. No bicycle dimensions, rider geometry or shared physics settings changed.

The controls-only demonstration stops before each bank, calls the cradle with a −0.4 radian world tilt and brake, levels the world before releasing the bank-side brake, and rolls aboard under speed feedback. It holds brake and tilts to +0.4 radians during the loaded crossing, then releases near the far bank. All world rotation still uses the normal bounded tilt input; the pilot never sets a world angle or changes body state. The second bank has a flat settling area before boarding. The original wall and ceiling control sequence then collects the remaining apples and reaches the door.

Validation at 120 Hz completed the route with all six apples in 76.90 seconds over 103.68 m. The first seat carried both wheels continuously over 1.782 m, with 6.25 seconds of braked support; the second carried both wheels over 1.853 m, with 6.567 seconds of braked support. Permanent tests require over 1.6 m of uninterrupted two-wheel supported motion and over 4 seconds of braked support on each cradle. The usual campaign replay also passes.

Each seat is independently replaced by a fixed platform at its authored starting pose and replayed with the exact successful recorded controls; neither modified route completes or reaches the far bank of the changed crossing. The existing removal tests independently remove each cradle and require a crash near that crossing. These negative tests establish that the demonstrated route needs both seats and their motion; they do not claim every possible player trajectory has been exhaustively ruled out.

Independent review re-ran all 18 focused tests with no blocking findings.
