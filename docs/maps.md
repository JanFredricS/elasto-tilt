# Campaign map routes and validation

Map coordinates are metres with positive y up. All routes below are **authored intentions**. A static shape check confirms bounds, distinct IDs, a clear bike spawn, and the particular Escher/Clockwork structure; it does not prove a bike can ride a route. A level earns `replay-passed` only after a recorded control sequence reaches `complete` with all apples in the actual physics engine. Visual and phone checks are separate.

Levels 1–9 have successful deterministic physics replays. Their stated special mechanics have varying evidence: the wall turns and swing gaps were exercised, while braking, a wheel catch, and interaction with the inverted weight were not forced by the passing routes. No map has visual or physical-device validation. These statuses must change only when the corresponding evidence exists. In particular, the Clockwork route must be shown both successful with reversal and blocked without platform motion before calling the final mechanic indispensable.
