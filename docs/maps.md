# Campaign map routes and validation

Map coordinates are metres with positive y up. The static checks cover rotated terrain and initial prop, swing, and platform geometry as well as IDs and bike spawn clearance. They do not prove a bike can ride a route. A level earns `replay-passed` only after a deterministic scripted controller reaches `complete` with all apples in the actual physics engine. Visual and phone checks are separate.

| # | Level | Mechanic and intended checkpoints | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Newton’s Orchard | Gently turn to roll right; collect apples at x=3.2 and 8.2; brake and touch exit at x=13.4. | **Replay-passed; browser complete**: deterministic physics test collected both apples and completed in about 10 s; Chrome playthrough also unlocked level 2. |
| 2 | One Wheel Wonder | Roll up the low ramp, use the brake to settle speed at its narrow lip, continue onto the terrace; collect terrace apple and exit. | **Replay-passed**: all apples and exit with brake applied at the lip. A true wheel pivot remains unproved. |
| 3 | The Hanging Garden | Collect left apple; cross on the wide swing centered at x≈8.4; collect swing apple; land on right floor, collect third apple and exit. | **Replay-passed; browser complete**: all apples and exit across the swing gap. A brake-assisted transfer remains unproved. |
| 4 | The Pendulum Mill | Use the short swing to reach the center island, then the long swing to reach right floor; collect apples at each safe stop and exit. Their different lengths give different physical responses, though the passing route does not require waiting for a period. | **Replay-passed; browser complete**: both gaps crossed; all apples and exit. |
| 5 | The Room on Its Side | Collect floor apple; turn toward +90° so right wall is downhill; collect wall apple; turn toward 180° so ceiling is downhill; collect ceiling apple and exit along ceiling. Curved inner corners keep the wheels in contact during rotation. | **Replay-passed**: all three apples and exit through +90° and 180°. |
| 6 | Newton’s Attic | Nudge the loose ball and cross the narrow static bridge; collect the bridge and far-side apples, then exit. There is no weight-operated bridge mechanism. | **Replay-passed**: all apples and exit; replay asserts the ball moves right, not that its movement changes access. |
| 7 | Escher’s Orchard | Follow continuous planar floor through two curved corners, up right wall, then along ceiling; collect one apple on each surface and exit on the ceiling. Impossible-looking stair imagery may decorate the room, but traversal uses only the visible surfaces. | **Replay-passed; browser complete and visually checked**: all three apples and exit through +90° and 180°. |
| 8 | The Contrary Conservatory | Watch the marked weight fall toward the ceiling; pass the central low gate, collect the two far apples, exit. The inverted object demonstrates the rule; it is not a required switch. | **Replay-passed**: all apples and exit; replay asserts the inverted weight rises. The route does not require touching it. |
| 9 | The Gravity Engine | Cross the swing over the pit, then turn toward the right wall to collect its apple and exit along the upper ledge. | **Replay-passed; browser complete**: gap, wall turn, all apples and exit. Brake use remains unproved. |
| 10 | The Clockwork Apple | Roll right along the start floor to collect the first apple and raise the single lift at x≈6.8. Board it, collect the high apple at (8.1, 2.65), then roll left to reverse the lift and return to the exit at x=−0.8. Timeline phase uses x-axis travel over 12 m and starts at zero on respawn. | **Replay-passed; browser complete and visually checked**: all apples and exit in about 13.5 s, with phase rising then falling below 0.15. A fixed-at-start lift fails to reach the high apple; a replay without the backward leg fails to finish. |

All ten levels have successful deterministic physics replays, with the Clockwork result and its two ablations recorded in `tests/clockwork.test.ts`. Chrome also completed levels 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, and 10; screenshots of Escher and Clockwork checkpoints were inspected. Details and evidence images are in [gameplay-validation.md](gameplay-validation.md). These establish the lift and reverse leg for the tested route; they are not a proof that every imaginable control sequence needs them. The wall turns and swing gaps were exercised, the brake was applied in level 2, and the inverted weight rose in level 8. A sustained wheel catch, upside-down hang, brake-assisted swing transfer, weight-operated bridge, and required inverted-weight interaction are not demonstrated by the campaign. Physical-device validation has not been performed.
