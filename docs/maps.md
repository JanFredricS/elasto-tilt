# Redesigned campaign routes

This replaces the short original routes. Coordinates are metres, positive y up. Every campaign map has a real Rapier replay (`src/dev/replay.ts`) that collect every apple and reach the exit using normal control commands. Timing below measures controlled demonstrations, not a forced minimum or speedrun record. Earlier review documents describe the superseded first layouts.

| Map | Route | Apples | Demonstrated distance / time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Newton’s First Lesson | *A body at rest stays at rest.* The opening lesson: tilt right a little to start rolling, then a gentle 8° hill with a long level run-out into the door; a back wall stops a wrong-way start. No brake needed, no hazards; its brake is soft (`brakeScale` .3), so grabbing it at speed off the hill top slows the rider instead of pitching them over the bars. For its first 8 s it alone shows the objective line. | 3 | 39 m / 18 s |
| Newton’s Orchard | Climb to a 4 m terrace, descend into a hollow, reach the far hill, brake and return home. | 5 | 98 m / 45 s |
| Galileo’s Ramp | *All bodies fall alike.* A gentle, hazard-free introduction to spring pads. Three inclined planes step down a hillside, and each ends in a 22° slide onto a soft brass kicker (`restitution` .5, the first authored value). The kicker tosses the rider gently onto the next terrace, past an apple hung over the toss. The third toss carries the rider out over a lower gallery, which leads back beneath the hill to a door below the spawn. Built to forgive a held tilt: the world can stay leaned up to about .3 rad all the way down (one narrow exception near .22; see its residuals). Its brake is very soft (`brakeScale` .1); the hint teaches never to brake in the air. See [galileos-ramp.md](galileos-ramp.md). | 5 | 141 m / 46 s |
| One Wheel Wonder | Brake the rear wheel against a gold stop and pivot up to the high apple; descend into the deep bowl, climb the far bank and return. | 6 | 58 s |
| The Hanging Garden | Upper garden, compact suspended crossing, sunken orchard, then cross the moving deck again on the return. | 5 | 98 m / 49 s |
| Foucault’s Hall | *The floor turns beneath the swing.* Three free cradle decks (4, 6 and 8 m; periods ≈ 4.1, 5.0, 5.7 s) swing on their own over water. Wait braked at each ramp top and let go as the deck tops out on the far side (sooner for the slow 8 m deck), so you meet it as it comes in against the bank’s stop. Ride it out braked, then tilt forward to hold it at its far apex and step off onto the next bank. Each deck has its own rhythm and a release window about a second wide. See [foucault-hall.md](foucault-hall.md). | 3 | about 52 m / 46 s |
| The Pendulum Mill | Call, board and brake on two compact cradles across moderate gaps, then climb the curved mill wall and return overhead. | 6 | 104 m / 77 s |
| Kepler’s Orbit | *Equal areas in equal times.* An egg-shaped loop ridden on its inside by turning the world, the Ouroboros the other way up: broad 5.5 m corners and a roof on top, broken by a hole into a door alcove; a tight 3 m end at the bottom whose foot is a brass pad set at 25° into the arc. Keep turning past upside-down to fly over the hole, and dive into the tight end so the pad throws you up to the perihelion apple; then stop turning on the climb to the roof and roll gently into the hole, falling *up* through it past the aphelion apple to the door. Falling in before both loop apples is an immediate crash. See [keplers-orbit.md](keplers-orbit.md). | 3 | 89 m / 26 s |
| The Room on Its Side | Sunken floor around a central divider, rounded end wall, ceiling journey home. | 6 | 92 m / 46 s |
| Newton’s Attic | *Set the mass in motion.* Nudge a small barrel into its floor socket (it seats and becomes floor), climb the ramp to the ridge cradle and lean into the 9.8 kg barrel until it pops over its 35° right lip (≈26–28° tilt with the bike pushing, ≈34° by tilt alone; the 68° back of the cradle holds it against any leftward tilt up to ≈1.15 rad), wait for it to drop into the matching round well and lock as the bridge, cross to the far apples, then return over the plug and up out of the cradle bowl past the door to the storage apple. | 6 | 108 m / 70 s |
| Escher’s Orchard | Lower gallery → east wall → roof → west wall → top of the former entry ceiling → inner-room reversal. | 8 | 111 m / 75 s |
| The Contrary Conservatory | Lower greenhouse, rising weights, end wall and roof; reverse out of the final roof pocket. | 7 | 102 m / 68 s |
| Torricelli’s Well | *Nature abhors a vacuum.* Brake-fall down a 10 m shaft into a cistern, ride a slow pendulum ferry across the water, push two rising floats off their beams so they seat in the vault as gold footholds, then turn the world over and ride the vault home over them to a door beside the shaft. See [torricellis-well.md](torricellis-well.md). | 5 | 85 m / 49 s |
| The Ice House | *Without friction, only gravity steers.* Polished ice (friction .1) and a .4 brake: off the six gold-striped mats the brake cannot hold the bike, so tilt is the throttle. Rock the world into three 40° ice faces to climb three stacked shelves (each tilted 8° back into a mat in its valley), ease over the slick roof and the slide's roller and lip under low vaults, tilt back down the 30° final slide or be thrown off its kicker, roll past the door up the end kicker for the last apple and reverse home. The mats are rest stops; tilt alone can ride it. No hazards. See [ice-house.md](ice-house.md). | 8 | 108 m / 75 s |
| The Gravity Engine | Compact hanging deck, long approach around an axle, wall climb and overhead gallery return. | 7 | 83 m / 56 s |
| The Clockwork Apple | Long approach raises a lift; collect the high apple and reverse the entire journey to lower it and return. | 5 | 74 m / 49 s |
| Leibniz’s Counterweight | *Every action has its reckoning.* The first map whose LEDGER (route clock) reads altitude (`timeAxis` (0, 1)): every metre climbed lowers the bridge a metre, every metre descended raises it, and the ledger saturates empty and full. Ride down into the 6.5 m cellar (free: the ledger is already empty) and climb back out to fill it, which parks the bridge flush with the kerbs either side of the pit (each kerb's .5 m climb pays back height lost to a hop or a hard stop); push a rising (inverted) ball east along the corridor ceiling until it floats up into its well and seats (a fairing east of the well stops it from overshooting); loop through the hall onto the ceiling and ride it upside down, down the fairing and over the plug to the door. The hall is deeper than the ledger, so the bridge can be re-parked from the east. No brake needed. See [leibniz-counterweight.md](leibniz-counterweight.md). | 5 | 191 m / 84 s |
| The Clockwork Wedge | Second time map. Riding right lowers a counterweight onto the door, which the spawn deck cannot reach. Ride west off the deck, come back beneath it and push a wooden wedge from under the deck to the weight (it seats as stone), ride over the weight and a one-way drop, loop a half-pipe onto the roof, ride left to lift the weight until a catch over the door stops you, then keep turning the world and fall onto the door. No brake needed. See [clockwork-wedge.md](clockwork-wedge.md). | 6 | 160 m / 79 s |
| The Other Side | Turn around the exposed end of one solid strip and return along its underside. | 5 | 61 m / 31 s |
| Huygens’ Return | One long, heavily damped pendulum: brake on its plank and turn the world to swing it a quarter turn up against a tower, ride up the wall and round a 180° crown, jump a saw-tooth spike trench off a kicker lip, tip the world back to re-park the plank, then ride its underside and swing home beneath the start shelf to the door. | 4 | 102 m / 58 s |
| The Pendulum Clock | *Tick, tock, and a time to cross.* Inside a giant clock: board the escapement’s first lift as it rests low and cross to the antiphase second lift only as their tops meet; slip beneath two red pallet bars (moving hazards, `TimePlatform.kind: 'hazard'`) that drop across the dial in antiphase every 6 s, stopping between them while the first drops behind; brake on the heavy-damped hand (mass .1, damping 400) under the BRAKE arrow and turn the world a quarter turn to ride it up to the 12 tower, climb its face, round the crown and roll to the door at the centre arbor. See [pendulum-clock.md](pendulum-clock.md). | 5 | 52 m / 50 s |
| Spiral Sanctuary | More than two inward circular turns, two airborne gap crossings, central door. | 9 | 192 m / 95 s |
| Euler’s Bridges | *Cross each bridge only once.* Seven bridge lifts on one RIVER clock that stops at full and at empty: ebb back to the wall behind the spawn, ride east over four rising canal bridges, slide down the chute (don't tilt back), roll on east into the far river wall to flood the clock (turn home short and the first river bridge is still under water), then ride home west over three sinking river bridges to the door. See [eulers-bridges.md](eulers-bridges.md). | 7 | 128 m / 47 s |
| Ampère’s Loop | *The current circles back.* Charge a curl to a vertical lip and fly straight up into a hanging 4 m ring, ride its inside a full lap, brake on its floor while tipping the world forward and drop across the gap onto the tower behind the jump, then ride the stone’s whole underside home upside down, round the outer bowl and over the crown into the start bowl. See [amperes-loop.md](amperes-loop.md). | 6 | 120 m / 43 s |
| Bernoulli’s Updraft | *Where the flow quickens, it lifts.* Keep turning the world round a loop and up a rising lintel’s underside until it throws you off upside down, turn gravity back up and ping-pong up a 30 m chimney between two brass wall pads, glance off a sloping hood onto the roof, ride it back over the start and down the far wall to the door. See [bernoulli-updraft.md](bernoulli-updraft.md). | 5 | 85 m / 16 s |
| Switchback Scaffold | Wall climb, free-air somersault, wheels-on-top landing, upper-storey return. | 6 | 182 m / 108 s |
| Pascal’s Presses | *Press here, rise there.* Two self-running presses in antiphase (12 s). Shove a wooden box crate onto press A as it rests low, ride it up, and push the crate over a sill into a hazard-floored pit at the top. Both presses are solid rams, so neither shaft is ever open. It seats only when level (`socket.angle`) and becomes the gold step to the ledge. Ride A down and back up, cross, ride press B (at the bottom as A tops out) to the press hall, and pass under three ceiling crush presses only while they rest up; a ram that catches you strikes the helmet. See [pascals-presses.md](pascals-presses.md). | 5 | 65 m / 79 s |
| The Hidden Way Home | Two opposing wall jumps, final wall apple, inverted ascent to a hidden ledge, original door. | 6 | about 387 m / 132 s |
| Hooke’s Springboard | Spring pads: slide into a brass pad, lean gravity forward in the throw to clear a spike pit, scrub the landing speed, then repeat over a wider pit to the door. The first throw forgives any lean from ≈ .35 to .8 rad; the second needs ≥ .5. See [hookes-springboard.md](hookes-springboard.md). | 4 | 59 m / 12 s |
| Hooke’s Ceiling | The sequel, upside down: start under a long slab with the world turned over (gravity map-up), ride Hooke’s two pads and pits as ceiling pads, leaning the world forward from π in each throw, scrub speed, curl round the slab’s rounded nose (world back from π to 0) onto its top face, and finish right-way-up with one ordinary floor throw over a 5 m spike pit to the door. See [hookes-ceiling.md](hookes-ceiling.md). | 7 | about 141 m / 40 s |
| The Piston Works | Self-running lifts (pairs of tyre-wide pistons on their own periods): board lift A as it rests low, ride up, drop through a half-pipe, ride B1 up and cross to B2 only as their tops meet, then ride lift C down its shaft for a mandatory apple and back up to the door. | 5 | 72 m / 63 s |
| Stairway to Heaven | Four giant steps: turn onto each vertical riser, ease over each crest, collect the summit apple, and descend home. | 8 | 197 m / 160 s |
| Noether’s Symmetry | One monument, the same under a half turn: swing a plank, spring a pit, ride a lift and seat a wedge along the top, round the far nose, and meet every mechanic again on the underside, then return to the door beside the start. | 10 | 215 m / 115 s |

The map 2 correction and measured one-wheel apple collection are documented in [one-wheel-fix.md](one-wheel-fix.md).

Acceptance coverage is in `tests/campaign-early.test.ts` and `tests/campaign-late.test.ts`. Static tests separately check rotated bounds and bike spawn clearance. Campaign tests reject idle, held rotation, and fixed-angle attempts as trivial quick solutions. They record actual wheel support on the swings. An independent reviewer removed each of the four swing decks and observed the unchanged pilot crash at its gap. Clockwork tests freeze the lift and omit the return leg; both experiments prevent the tested route from completing.

Escher now involves a complete gravity circuit and a shared ceiling/floor, with a continuous low tunnel around each bend and a reverse detour. Its geometry remains planar; it is not a topologically impossible world or a teleport system. Newton’s Attic’s ridge barrel is a required plug: props with a `socket` lock in place once they come to rest there. Loose props are harmless to the helmet. The Conservatory’s inverted weights still respond physically without operating a mandatory gate. Sustained upside-down hanging and brake-assisted free-wheel swing capture still need dedicated campaign challenges. See [map residuals](maps-residuals.md).

Browser gameplay evidence is recorded independently in [gameplay-v2.md](gameplay-v2.md). Full route overview is available using MAP or M, which pauses simulation. Physical phone sensor feel and sustained mobile performance remain hardware checks.
